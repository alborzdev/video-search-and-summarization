# Preserve operational trust through state and provenance

The application will make degraded Sources explicit, retain an audit trail of security, configuration, Alert, note, export, and retention actions, and connect operational metrics back to Events and Evidence. Cross-camera identity continuity will be claimed only when a configured and validated tracking Capability supports it; similarity search alone will never be presented as proof that two sightings are the same person or vehicle.
