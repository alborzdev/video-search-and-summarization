# One general application across operational Scenarios

CTAILabs Vision Intelligence will provide one broadly useful application across warehouse, traffic, and future operational Scenarios. A Scenario is primarily different footage and business context; optional configuration may adjust sources, rules, or models, but the core navigation, workflows, and available capabilities remain consistent. This avoids maintaining curated industry-specific interfaces while allowing agentic development to adapt exceptional requirements quickly.
