# Organize the application around operator work

The Showcase will organize its primary navigation around Operations, Investigate, and Manage rather than exposing Search, Alerts, Dashboard, and Video Management as equal NVIDIA feature surfaces. Alerts will appear in the live Operations flow and remain available as evidence during Investigation; model selection stays in CTAILabs-managed Scenario Templates and engineering diagnostics. This makes the same application credible for daily analysts and understandable during guided demonstrations without hiding its underlying capabilities.
