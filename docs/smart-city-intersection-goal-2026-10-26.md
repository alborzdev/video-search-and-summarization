## Design commitments

- Normal flow is the persistent base. Episodes inject finite changes, then release their owned actors/obstacles/overrides. They do not reset the whole stage between visitors.
- Initial catalog candidates: movement demand, approach queue/blocked junction, pedestrian waiting and crossing, curb/loading obstruction, and a stopped vehicle obstructing a crossing. Final choices require actual scene and analytics coverage.
- Each episode states the city's question, observable evidence, useful action and limits. A staged event label is simulation control information, not an AI detection.
- VSS derives detections and map observations from camera images. Actor ground truth can evaluate the result but cannot supply invented analytics.
- Keep camera calibration and map revisions explicit. Experimental MV3DT behavior must not be described as qualified fused counts.
- Semantic history belongs to a finite named demonstration interval. Old repeated loops should not silently dominate the current booth search. Existing history is preserved.
